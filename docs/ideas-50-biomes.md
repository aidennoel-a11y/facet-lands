# 50 biome ideas

None of these repeat earlier lists or anything in the game.

## Warm and dry (1–15)
1. Mediterranean scrub: olive trees, dark cypress spires, wild lavender and thyme on dry hills by the sea
2. Cork-oak pasture: big scattered oaks with stripped trunks over open grazing ground
3. Eucalyptus forest: tall, pale, peeling gum trees, a sharp scent, fierce fires
4. Red outback: rusty red sand, rings of spiky grass, termite towers taller than you
5. Fynbos: a heath of giant protea flowers and fine bushes that burns and blooms again
6. Chaparral: hills choked with dense, tough evergreen shrubs, hard to push through
7. Sagebrush steppe: silver-blue sage flats under the mountains, smelling of rain
8. Gravel desert: a flat plain of dark polished pebbles, nothing growing
9. Fog desert: a coastal desert kept alive only by sea fog: lichens and strange low plants
10. White gypsum dunes: blinding white sand that stays cool underfoot
11. Yardangs: desert ridges carved by the wind into long hull shapes all pointing one way
12. Crystal desert: gypsum crystals spearing up out of the ground
13. Slickrock domes: smooth, swirled sandstone humps you can walk all over
14. Dragon's-blood island: dry, rocky islands of umbrella-shaped trees with red sap
15. Spiny forest: weird octopus-armed trees and thorny trunks in red earth

## Grass and open country (16–23)
16. Pampas: a sea of tall grass topped with feathery white plumes
17. Tallgrass prairie: grass taller than you, hiding everything a few metres off
18. Prairie potholes: grassland dotted with hundreds of little ponds full of ducks
19. Oak savanna: lone spreading oaks on open grass
20. Longleaf pine savanna: tall pines far apart over wiregrass, kept open by fire
21. Cedar glades: bare limestone flats with junipers and spring flowers in the cracks
22. Pitcher-plant savanna: wet grassland thick with carnivorous plants
23. Jacaranda savanna: purple-blossomed trees scattered over the grass in spring

## Wet and water (24–30)
24. Floodplain grassland: open country under water half the year, alive with birds
25. Inland delta: a river spreading out into the dry country, a maze of reed channels and islands
26. Blackwater forest: tea-dark rivers through forest flooded for months
27. Ghost forest: silver dead trees standing in salt marsh where the sea crept in
28. Thousand-island lake: a great lake crowded with small wooded islands
29. Salt-crust coast: flat, glittering salt flats right by the sea
30. Hanging gardens: canyon walls dripping with water, ferns and flowers growing out of them

## Mountains and cold (31–42)
31. Páramo: cold, misty high grassland with giant shaggy rosette plants
32. Puna: a high, dry, windswept plateau with salt lakes and pink flamingos
33. Afro-alpine moor: tree-sized groundsels and giant lobelias above the clouds
34. Bristlecone heights: ancient, twisted pines on bare white rock
35. Sky islands: forested mountains rising like islands out of the desert
36. Rhododendron hills: whole mountainsides of flowering rhododendrons
37. Rime forest: trees caked white with frozen fog all winter on a cold, misty mountain
38. Ice field: a great sheet of ice with rocky peaks poking up through it
39. Lichen woodland: open spruce over a thick white carpet of lichen
40. Drumlin fields: hundreds of smooth, egg-shaped hills all lying the same way
41. Esker country: long, winding gravel ridges snaking between lakes
42. Southern beech forest: twisted beeches hung with pale beard lichen

## Strange and rare (43–50)
43. Limestone pavement: flat grey rock split into blocks, ferns growing deep in the cracks
44. Moss lava fields: a thick green moss carpet over black lava
45. Loess hills: steep hills of fine wind-blown dust, cut by deep gullies
46. Red-earth gullies: bare red soil eroded into sharp, deep ravines
47. Rainbow eucalyptus grove: trees whose bark peels in stripes of green, orange and purple
48. Flowering cactus bloom: a cactus desert that flowers all at once for a few days
49. Ice-cave forest: woods on top of caves breathing cold air, with ice all summer below
50. Bamboo-and-mist gorge: steep gorge walls covered in bamboo, waterfalls in the mist
