# 200 more ideas: biomes, exploration, the Lost Keep and progression

None of these are in the game, and none repeat the earlier lists (docs/ideas-200.md, docs/ideas-200-b.md). Checked against the game's biomes, landmarks, ruins, cave kinds, great beasts and crafting tiers.

## New biomes (1–40)
1. Tundra: flat, frozen, moss and lichen, bare of trees, wind always blowing
2. Taiga: dark spruce forest on the edge of the tundra, deep snow half the year
3. Steppe: endless dry grass, cold winters, wild horses in big herds
4. Fjord coast: sheer cliffs dropping into narrow arms of the sea
5. Boreal bog: spongy ground of moss over water, stunted black spruce
6. Alpine meadow: flower-filled grass high above the treeline, below the snow
7. Cloud forest: misty mountainsides, trees dripping with moss and orchids
8. Karst country: grey limestone towers and pinnacles rising out of green valleys
9. Salt marsh: tidal grass flats cut by winding muddy channels
10. Coastal dunes: sand hills with marram grass behind the beaches
11. Chalk downs: rolling white-soiled hills with short grass and white cliffs at the sea
12. Heathland: purple heather and gorse on poor, sandy hills
13. Petrified forest: a dry valley of stone tree trunks lying where they fell
14. Ash plains: grey land buried by an old eruption, slowly greening
15. Obsidian fields: black glass flows that glitter in the sun
16. Geyser basin: a valley full of steaming vents, colourful pools and mud pots
17. Sulfur flats: yellow crusted ground, the air stinging, near volcanic country
18. Mud volcano field: little cones bubbling grey mud
19. Ice shelf: flat white ice running out over the sea, cracking at its edge
20. Frozen lake country: hundreds of small lakes that freeze solid each winter
21. Birch tundra edge: crooked dwarf birches in the wind
22. Monsoon forest: dry and bare half the year, flooded and green the other half
23. Baobab savanna: huge swollen trees spaced across the grass
24. Acacia thorn scrub: thorny bushes that slow you and snag clothes
25. Oasis: a pool and palms in the middle of the deep desert
26. Canyonlands: a maze of deep slot canyons cut into red rock
27. Hoodoo valley: tall rock spires with balanced caps
28. Painted desert: bands of red, purple and white clay hills
29. Cactus forest: tall branching cacti, birds nesting in them
30. Rainforest river delta: many channels, floating islands of plants
31. Lily lagoon: shallow warm water covered with giant lily pads you can stand on
32. Fungal forest (on the surface): towering mushrooms in a damp, dark valley
33. Ancient oak wood: huge old oaks, ivy, deep shade, little grows under them
34. Autumn maple forest: always red and gold, even in summer
35. Willow river valley: weeping willows along slow green rivers
36. Moorland: open, windswept, peaty uplands with standing water
37. Sea-stack coast: towers of rock standing in the surf
38. Coral atoll islands: rings of white sand round a calm lagoon
39. Black sand volcanic islands: palms on black beaches under a smoking peak
40. Floating-rock valley: huge boulders balanced on thin necks of rock (a fantasy biome, rare)
